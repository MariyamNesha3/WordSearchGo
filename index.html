The Challenges

Identifying key usability and engagement challenges to create a comfortable, accessible, and rewarding word-game experience for players across different age groups.

E A M C

Easy to Pick Up,
Easy to Return

Players may open the game for a few minutes between other activities and return later. The experience needed to make it easy to understand the current puzzle, resume gameplay, and enjoy short sessions without a steep learning curve.

A Moment of Calm

For women aged 30+, gaming can be a way to unwind between daily responsibilities. The challenge was creating a relaxing word-puzzle experience that offers a mental break without adding pressure or unnecessary complexity.

Mental Stimulation
Without the Stress

Word puzzles appeal to players who enjoy learning, vocabulary, and keeping their minds active. The challenge was balancing cognitive stimulation with a comfortable difficulty curve that feels rewarding rather than frustrating.

Comfort Across
Every Session

Players need an interface that remains comfortable during both short and extended sessions. The challenge was maintaining readable typography, clear visual contrast, intuitive touch targets, and consistent interactions throughout the game.
